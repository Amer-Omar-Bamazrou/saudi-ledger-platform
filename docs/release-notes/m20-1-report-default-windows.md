# Release note — reports now open on YOUR financial year

**Applies to:** every report page with a date range (16 pages).
**Nature of change:** the period a report shows **when you first open it**
changes. **Your figures are not restated** — the same dates still produce the
same numbers; only the dates a report *starts on* are different.

---

## What you will notice

Until now, every report opened on **January to December of the current year**,
whatever your financial year actually is. That was an assumption, not a
setting — and if your year starts in any other month, every report opened on
the wrong period without saying so.

From this release:

| Your situation | Reports now open on |
| --- | --- |
| Financial year **declared** in Company Settings | Your **current financial year** (Gregorian or Hijri) |
| Financial year **not declared** | The **last 12 months**, with a note on the report saying so and a link to set it |

You can still pick any dates you like — nothing about the date controls
changed, only the starting values.

## Why reports may look different the first time you open them

If you never set a financial year, reports used to open on Jan–Dec and will now
open on the last 12 months. The figures for the window shown are correct in
both cases; the **window itself** is what moved, with no action on your part.
This note exists because a report that silently changes what it shows on open
is worth telling you about — even when the change is the honest one.

**To pin reports to your financial year:** Company Settings → *Fiscal year
starts* → choose the month. Reports follow it immediately.

## What did NOT change

- Figures for any explicitly chosen date range — identical before and after.
- The VAT return, ZATCA documents, the GL, and all posted records.
- The Balance Sheet's "as of" date (it opens on today, as before).
