# Release note — income statement classification corrected

**Applies to:** all organizations, all historical periods.
**Nature of change:** a display defect corrected. **This is not a restatement of
your results.**

---

## What you will notice

The next time you open or export an **income statement**, figures for past
periods will differ from what the same report showed before this release:

| Line | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | Understated — often **zero**, even in periods with invoices | Your actual sales revenue |
| **Expenses** | Understated by the **same amount** | Your actual expenses |
| **Net profit / net income** | **Correct** | **Unchanged — identical** |

The **balance sheet** will also show **VAT Payable** as a liability line where it
previously did not appear, and retained earnings will reflect the corrected
revenue and expense classification. Assets, liabilities and equity totals are
unchanged, and the statement still balances.

**No other report changes.** VAT returns, the Zakat base, cash flow, AR/AP aging
and customer statements are all unaffected — their figures are computed from your
invoices, bills and transactions, not from the classification that was corrected.

---

## Why your bottom line has not moved

This is the important part, and it is why we describe it as a display defect
rather than a restatement.

Invoice postings were reaching the general ledger correctly — the amounts, the
double entry, the balances and the tax figures were all right. What was wrong was
only how one line was **labelled** when the income statement grouped it.

An invoice's sales revenue was being grouped with expenses instead of with
revenue. Because it was recorded as a credit, it appeared there as a *negative*
expense — which subtracted exactly as much from the expense total as it should
have added to the revenue total. The two errors cancelled precisely.

The consequence: **revenue and expenses were each understated by the same
amount, and net profit was always correct.** Nothing you filed, paid or reported
based on net profit, VAT or Zakat was affected.

---

## What this means for you

- **Comparing to an older export?** A statement you exported before this release
  will show different revenue and expense figures from the same report today.
  The new figures are the correct ones.
- **Filed anything based on these reports?** VAT returns and Zakat are computed
  separately and did not change, so nothing you have filed is affected. If you
  used the income statement's *revenue* line for another purpose, that figure has
  been corrected upward.
- **Nothing is required of you.** The correction applies automatically to every
  period, past and future. No re-entry, no re-approval, no adjusting entries.

---

## One thing that is new

Every organization now has a small **standard chart of accounts** (Accounts
Receivable, Sales Revenue, VAT Payable, and so on) visible on the Categories
page. These accounts are what the corrected classification uses.

- You **can** rename them and edit their Arabic names to match how your business
  refers to them.
- You **cannot** delete them or change their type, because your financial
  statements depend on them.
- Your own categories are unaffected and continue to work exactly as before.

Manual journal entries now require every line to name an account. A line with no
account cannot appear on the income statement or balance sheet, which is the same
defect corrected above, reached from a different direction.
